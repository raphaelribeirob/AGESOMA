# AGESOMA Operations — Wedge, Economics and Rollout

## Investment thesis

Software disguised as a service: contract for recovered money and operating reliability instead of seats. AI reduces unit labor cost, while deterministic reconciliation, quality control, approvals and evidence make outputs accountable.

This is a strategy to validate, not a forecast or guarantee of trillion-dollar valuation.

## ICP and one starting workflow

Start with Brazilian mid-market manufacturers, distributors or shippers with repeat freight expenses, digitized transport contracts and enough CT-e/invoice volume to compare. The economic buyer should be CFO, controller or head of logistics.

**Only initial offer:** outsourced freight overcharge detection and recovery. Do not launch a generic ERP, CFO agent or broad BPO offering before repeatable recovery economics are proven.

## 14–30 day discovery/pilot flow

1. Secure a data-processing agreement and a narrow, signed service scope.
2. Collect export of contracted freight tariffs, supporting rules, CT-e and invoices for a limited period.
3. Determine whether evidence/data coverage is sufficient before charging for automation.
4. Reconcile charges deterministically; create explainable discrepancies with contract and document references.
5. Have an accountable human validate candidate cases.
6. Secure a concrete customer authorization before disputing externally.
7. Submit through an approved channel when that integration exists; otherwise the human submits.
8. Verify cash received or customer-approved offset with independent settlement evidence.
9. Invoice only the contractually permitted fee and report the entire funnel, including rejected cases.

## Unit economics (targets, not measured results)

- Gross margin: demonstrate a credible path toward 60%+ after operator labor, AI, API, infra, review and support costs.
- Proof: three paying pilot customers with actual verified recoveries.
- Conversion: measure cases detected -> evidence complete -> approved -> submitted -> recovered.
- Quality: track false positives and disputes; never inflate recovered dollars using findings alone.
- Account expansion: prevention subscription only after proving recovered value and repeat leakage patterns.
- Contract terms: explicit fee base and cap, permitted offsets, taxes, reversals, refunds and client dispute process.

## No-go conditions

- No proven mismatch under governing contract terms.
- Unverifiable source documents or insufficient rights to process them.
- No independent settlement proof.
- No approved scope for third-party communications or commitments.
- Loss-leading operator hours with no path to acceptable contribution margins.
- Client requires the service to autonomously move funds or decide regulated legal disputes.

## Expansion sequence

1. Freight Audit Recovery.
2. Freight Leakage Prevention.
3. Invoice / AP audit and duplicate-payment prevention.
4. Managed reconciliation and collections under clear permissions.
5. Wider enterprise operations, only where the same evidence-and-execution primitives apply.

## Segregation of interests

AGESOMA Personal remains a separate potential offer and should not be merged into this service contract. Shared runtime infrastructure does not mean shared customer data or cross-product permissions.
