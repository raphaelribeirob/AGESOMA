# AGESOMA Product Scope

## One job

AGESOMA exists for one purpose: organize the operation of a small or medium business so work moves without the owner having to coordinate everything manually.

Internally we use the shorthand **JARVIS for SMEs**.

The product must always be able to answer:

1. What needs to happen now?
2. Who is responsible?
3. What is blocked?
4. What can AGESOMA resolve digitally instead of occupying an employee?
5. What actually needs the owner's decision?
6. What was completed and what result was proven?

## Canonical operating model

`Owner -> AGESOMA -> employee or digital execution -> follow-up -> result`

AGESOMA knows the team, roles, responsibilities, active work, blockers, owner decisions and verified results.

HERMES is an internal digital executor controlled by AGESOMA. It is not a product surface, team member, brand, mode or customer-facing concept.

## Canonical owner experience

`Início | Equipe | Trabalho | Resultados`

Everything else is secondary infrastructure or just-in-time context.

## Required product objects

- Company context
- Team member
- Role and responsibilities
- Work assignment
- Work status
- Owner decision
- Verified result
- Business memory

Every active piece of work must have one accountable executor: a human team member or AGESOMA's internal digital execution.

## Non-goals

AGESOMA must not become:

- an agent marketplace;
- an AI-agent builder;
- a workflow studio;
- a CRM replacement;
- a payroll or HRIS product;
- a recruiting product;
- an employee-surveillance product;
- a project-management suite with AI added;
- a collection of vertical products such as AGESOMA Consórcio, AGESOMA Accounting or AGESOMA Insurance;
- a dashboard whose primary purpose is showing AI activity.

CRM, messaging, calendars, files and specialized methods may be used internally when they help coordinate work. They do not become separate product purposes.

## Product test

A feature belongs in AGESOMA only if it improves at least one of these outcomes:

- understanding who does what;
- deciding who should do a piece of work;
- removing work from employees when it can be completed digitally;
- following progress and blockers;
- reducing unnecessary owner intervention;
- proving that work actually finished.

If a proposed feature does none of these, it is outside the product scope.
