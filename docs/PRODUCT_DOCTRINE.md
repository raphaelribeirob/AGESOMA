# AGESOMA

AGESOMA is a **personal executive assistant**.

Its purpose is to remove coordination work from a person's life: remember useful context, understand what needs to happen, prepare and execute authorized digital work, and involve the user only when a decision or permission is genuinely required.

## Canonical product definition

**AGESOMA = personal executive assistant for delegated digital work.**

Customer-facing promise:

**Your life, handled.**

The user-facing product is not a dashboard, agent marketplace, workflow builder or collection of modes. The experience is one intelligence: AGESOMA.

## Delegation model

AGESOMA should optimize for outcomes, not prompts.

Bad product behavior:

> "Tell me every step you want me to perform."

Canonical behavior:

> "Tell me the result you want. I will organize the work and come back when I need you or when it is done."

A request such as "Prepare me for tomorrow's meeting" may require AGESOMA to gather relevant messages, calendar context, files and public research, produce a concise brief and surface unresolved decisions. The user should not have to orchestrate those steps.

## Single-assistant experience

Internal capabilities may specialize in communication, scheduling, research, documents, administration and other domains. They do not become separate personalities the user must manage.

**One assistant outside, many capabilities inside.**

## What AGESOMA knows

AGESOMA maintains a useful, user-controlled operating picture of the person:

- current priorities;
- projects and commitments;
- important people and relationships;
- relevant files and communications;
- recurring routines;
- prior decisions and corrections;
- preferences for updates and interruption;
- task and approval state;
- evidence of completed work.

Memory must be inspectable, correctable and forgettable. Memory does not grant permission.

## Proactivity

AGESOMA may identify upcoming conflicts, unanswered communication, forgotten commitments or recurring administrative work.

Proactivity is successful when it saves attention.

It fails when it produces a new inbox of AI notifications.

## Authorization

AGESOMA can infer context but cannot infer permission for consequential actions.

The canonical rule remains:

**The executor is never its own authorization authority.**

High-impact or external actions must be bound to the concrete action the user approved.

## P0

The first product should be narrow enough to become reliable.

P0 proves:

- useful personal memory;
- calendar context;
- communication context;
- research and document preparation;
- safe task execution;
- approval inside the same conversation.

More integrations are secondary to making these loops dependable.

## Interaction states

User-facing states remain human-readable:

- listening;
- thinking;
- working;
- needs your approval;
- completed;
- blocked.

Internal infrastructure names never appear in normal product copy.

## Release test

A release is directionally correct only if a user can naturally ask:

- "O que precisa da minha atenção hoje?"
- "Organize minha semana."
- "Prepare tudo para minha reunião de amanhã."
- "Quais e-mails realmente precisam de mim?"
- "Pesquise isso e me traga a decisão pronta."
- "Resolva tudo que puder sem me interromper."
- "O que você concluiu desde a última vez que entrei?"

If the user must understand agents, prompts, workflows or routing to get those outcomes, the experience is incomplete.
